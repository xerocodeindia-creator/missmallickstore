ABOUT OUR BUSINESS

About Miss Mallick Store

Miss Mallick Store is a micro enterprise registered under the Government of India's Udyam registration system. The business is based in the Basirhat / Swarupnagar area of North 24 Parganas, West Bengal.

Our listed business activity is in services, including general household maintenance and routine cleaning-related tasks. Customers are encouraged to contact us to discuss their specific requirements, availability and pricing before any service is agreed.

How we work

  • We discuss the requested work and scope before confirming a booking.
  • We communicate the agreed price and timing before work begins.
  • We aim to treat enquiries respectfully and handle customer information only for legitimate communication and service purposes.

For enquiries, please visit our Contact page. This website describes the business and does not itself confirm service availability or a booking.
